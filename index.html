Ashfall: The Beginning

Survival

Cross into Ashfall—an unforgiving, persistent open-world warzone where tactical precision meets raw survival. Blending the emergent base-building and territorial warfare of Rust, the vast, unpredictable open-world tension of DayZ, and the high-stakes gunplay and extraction mechanics of Escape from Tarkov, Ashfall pushes realism and player-driven survival to the limit.

Enter hostile sectors alone or with a squad to scavenge high-tier weaponry, medical supplies, and rare components. Every engagement is governed by authentic milsim mechanics—realistic ballistics, modular weapon customization, stamina management, and a punishing health system where a single misstep can cost you everything you brought in.
